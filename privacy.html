01. Introduction

Welcome to Beyond Thyme Supper Club ("Beyond Thyme", "we", "our", or "us"). Beyond Thyme is a private, offline temporal supper club operating exclusive multi-course dining experiences in private villas for 25 invited members per session.

We respect your fundamental right to privacy and are committed to safeguarding the personal data you entrust to us when accessing our website, calibrating vibe vectors, reserving dining slots, acquiring digital QR tickets, or participating in our secret society events. This Privacy Policy outlines our data governance practices, security protocols, and your legal rights under applicable privacy laws.

02. Information We Collect

To provide seamless vetting, seat allocation, digital ticketing, and personalized dining experiences, we collect specific categories of personal information:

  • Google Identity Profile Data: Full Name, Primary Email Address, Profile Picture URL, and unique Google Account Identifier collected during Google OAuth authentication.
  • Member Passport & Vetting Data: Phone number (for WhatsApp match drop notifications), member pseudonym/alias, and emergency contact details.
  • Dining Preferences & Safety Data: Food preferences, strict dietary restrictions (e.g. Vegetarian, Vegan, Jain), food allergy disclosures, and beverage pairings.
  • Reservation & Ticket Metadata: Booking serial IDs (`BT-YYYY-MM-XXXXXX`), reserved seat numbers, seat quantity, payment transaction identifiers, and check-in timestamps.
  • Device & Telemetry Data: IP address, browser type, operating system version, resolution, screen dimensions, and time-zone calibration parameters.
  • Cookies & Local Storage: Authentication tokens, local session states, and guestbook submissions.

03. How We Use Your Information

We utilize your information strictly for legitimate operational purposes required to run an exclusive, curated supper club:

  • Event Bookings & Seat Vetting: Processing seat allocations, verifying member identities, and matching guests for harmonious 6-stranger table arrangements.
  • Digital QR Ticket Generation: Creating encrypted digital tickets with unique verification QR codes stored safely under "My Tickets" and sent via email/WhatsApp.
  • Event Communication: Dispatching secret villa location drops 2 hours before dinner, event schedule updates, and gate check-in instructions.
  • Culinary Customization: Communicating allergy requirements and dietary constraints to our private executive culinary team.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: Preventing duplicate check-ins at villa door stations and safeguarding against unauthorized access.

04. Google Authentication

Beyond Thyme uses official Google OAuth 2.0 via Firebase Authentication for secure member sign-in and account access.

Password Non-Storage Disclosure: Beyond Thyme NEVER sees, processes, or stores your Google account password. Authentication credentials are handled exclusively by Google's OAuth infrastructure. When you sign in, Google provides Beyond Thyme with a cryptographically signed auth token verifying your identity.

05. Payment Security

All monetary transactions on Beyond Thyme (including seat reservations at ₹3,500 per seat) are securely processed through third-party PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways (Razorpay).

Card Detail Disclosure: Beyond Thyme does not store or process raw credit card numbers, debit card numbers, CVVs, or net banking passwords on our servers. Razorpay handles all billing data securely in compliance with RBI guidelines and PCI-DSS Level 1 standards.

06. Cookies & Analytics

We use essential session cookies and local storage tokens to keep you logged in, preserve your local ticket wallet cache, and store your temporal vibe calibration vector.

We do not use intrusive third-party advertising tracking cookies. Any analytical cookies used serve exclusively to monitor website performance and optimize page load speeds.

07. Data Storage Architecture

Your personal data and ticket records are stored in high-security cloud infrastructure powered by Google Cloud & Firebase Cloud Firestore.

  • Cloud Firestore: Stores structured ticket documents, seat matrices, and vetting entries encrypted at rest.
  • Firebase Authentication: Manages secure user session state and OAuth tokens.
  • Firebase Storage: Stores generated PDF/PNG ticket assets and event artifacts.

08. Data Security & Encryption

Beyond Thyme enforces multi-layered technical security controls to protect your personal data:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS): All web traffic is encrypted in transit using 256-bit TLS/SSL encryption.
  • Granular Firestore Rules: Database access is strictly governed by authenticated user UID checks so users can only view their own tickets.
  • Restricted Admin Access: Admin door check-in features are protected by multi-factor admin email verification (`beyondthyme.in@gmail.com`).

09. Your Privacy Rights

You maintain full ownership and control over your personal data. You have the right to:

  • Access & Review: View all tickets and profile information associated with your account under "My Tickets".
  • Correct & Update: Request updates to dietary preferences, allergies, or contact information prior to dinner sessions.
  • Data Erasure: Request permanent deletion of your profile and reservation history by emailing our privacy team.

10. Third-Party Integrations

Beyond Thyme integrates with select trusted third-party service providers essential for platform operations:

  • Google Authentication: User identity verification.
  • Firebase Services: Cloud database, storage, and hosting.
  • Razorpay: Payment processing gateway.
  • WhatsApp Business API: Private location drops and invitation notifications.
