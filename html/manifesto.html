MANIFESTO

MORE THAN A MEAL.
A MEMORY IN THE MAKING.

We believe the best moments rarely happen according to plan.
They happen somewhere between the first hello and the last bite.
Around tables where strangers become familiar, stories are exchanged,
music brings back memories, and nobody notices how late it has become.

Beyond Thyme was created for those moments.

Each edition is a thoughtfully curated experience built around a new story —
expressed through food, setting, music, conversation and little surprises along the way.

No two tables need to feel the same.

Because the food may bring us together.
But it is everything around it that we remember.
